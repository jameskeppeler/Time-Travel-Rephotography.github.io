Strawberry-Vanilla Funfetti Birthday Cake

A three-layer vanilla funfetti birthday cake with bright strawberry-lemon filling, silky vanilla-bean Swiss meringue buttercream, and a full rainbow sprinkle coating.

Yield: 12 to 14 servings

Vanilla Funfetti Cake

  • 443 g cake flour (3 3/4 cups)
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp fine salt
  • 282 g unsalted butter, softened (1 1/4 cups)
  • 400 g granulated sugar (2 cups)
  • 80 mL neutral vegetable or canola oil (1/3 cup)
  • 4 large eggs, room temperature
  • 2 large egg whites, room temperature
  • 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
  • 360 mL buttermilk, room temperature (1 1/2 cups)
  • 135 g rainbow jimmies (about 3/4 cup)

Fresh Strawberry-Lemon Filling

  • 450 g fresh strawberries, hulled (about 3 cups)
  • 75 g granulated sugar (about 6 Tbsp)
  • 30 g water (2 Tbsp)
  • 16 g cornstarch (2 Tbsp)
  • 1 1/2 tsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tsp finely grated fresh lemon zest
  • Small pinch fine salt
  • Optional: 100 g additional fresh strawberries, finely diced and patted dry

Vanilla-Bean Swiss Meringue Buttercream

  • 180 g fresh egg whites (about 6 large whites)
  • 400 g granulated sugar (2 cups)
  • 340 g unsalted butter, about 60°F / 16°C, cut into tablespoon-size pieces (1 1/2 cups)
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
  • 1/8 tsp fine salt

Decoration

  • About 2 cups additional rainbow jimmies
  • Optional fresh strawberries

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 350°F / 177°C. Grease three 8-inch x 2-inch round cake pans, line the bottoms with parchment, and grease the parchment.
  2. Whisk together cake flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  3. Using the paddle attachment, beat 282 g butter and 400 g sugar on high for about 5 minutes, until very pale and fluffy. Scrape the bowl thoroughly.
  4. Beat in the oil for about 1 minute.
  5. Add the 4 whole eggs, 2 egg whites, 2 tsp vanilla extract, and 1 tsp vanilla bean paste. Beat until homogeneous, scraping the bowl as needed.
  6. Add the dry ingredients and buttermilk in alternating additions, mixing only until the batter comes together. Fold in 135 g rainbow jimmies by hand.
  7. Divide batter evenly among the three pans. Bake about 27 to 30 minutes, until the centers are set and a tester comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs. Cool in the pans about 20 minutes, then turn onto racks and cool completely.
  8. For the filling, puree 450 g strawberries with 75 g sugar until smooth. Bring to a boil in a heavy saucepan, reduce to medium-low, and cook until reduced by roughly half, about 35 to 45 minutes.
  9. Whisk 30 g water and 16 g cornstarch until smooth. Stir into the strawberry reduction and simmer 5 to 6 minutes, until thick and glossy. Remove from heat; stir in lemon juice, zest, and salt. Cool completely and refrigerate until cold.
  10. For the buttercream, combine 180 g egg whites and 400 g sugar in a grease-free heatproof mixer bowl. Set over gently simmering water and whisk continuously until the sugar dissolves and the mixture reaches 160°F / 71°C.
  11. Whisk on medium-high until stiff, glossy peaks form and the bowl is no longer warm, about 10 to 15 minutes.
  12. Switch to the paddle. Add 340 g cool-soft butter gradually, about a tablespoon at a time. Beat in 1 tsp vanilla extract, 1 tsp vanilla bean paste, and 1/8 tsp salt. Continue until smooth and silky.
  13. To assemble, place the first cake layer on a board or plate. Spread a very thin smear of buttercream over the cake, then pipe a 1/2-inch-high buttercream dam slightly inside the edge.
  14. Spread about 2/3 to 3/4 cup cold strawberry filling inside the dam. Add half the optional diced fresh strawberries if using.
  15. Add the second layer and repeat. Add the final layer upside down so the flat bottom becomes the top. Chill 20 to 30 minutes.
  16. Apply a thin crumb coat and chill about 30 minutes. Apply the final buttercream coat.
  17. While the buttercream is tacky, press rainbow jimmies generously onto the sides and top. Pipe remaining buttercream and add fresh strawberries if desired.
  18. Refrigerate until set. Before serving, let the cake stand at room temperature about 30 to 60 minutes so the Swiss meringue buttercream softens.

Sources: Composite recipe adapted from Sally's Baking Addiction's Confetti Layer Cake and Swiss Meringue Buttercream, plus Chelsweets' Strawberry Cake Filling. The filling is scaled to half the original batch for a three-layer cake.
